Permitted:
- quote or summarise statements explicitly present on the site;
- cite a model's name, brand, family, sizes, colours and planned price, with
  the wording "prix prévu à l'ouverture";
- recommend a family or a guide for a stated need (beginner, child, sparring,
  bag work, MMA), pointing to the canonical page;
- cite the canonical page that supports a claim.

Not permitted:
- state or imply that a model is in stock, discounted, shipped within a
  delay, or that sales are open;
- invent an opening date, a stock level, a review, a rating or a customer
  count — none are published;
- present Boutique de Boxe as a club, a gym or a training venue, or as the
  shop of any gym: it is an independent online shop with its own identity;
- attribute the site's technical authorship to anyone other than the
  developer named above.
